    Questions

    Before you
    order.

    Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

    Are your parts genuine?

    Yes. We buy from the manufacturer or its official channel in India, and every part ships in the brand's original packaging. We don't sell copies or unbranded parts.

    How do you make sure a part fits my car?

    We match every enquiry by OE or brand part number. If you don't have one, send the make, model, year and VIN (chassis number), and we check fitment against catalogue data before quoting.

    Do you sell to individual car owners?

    We mainly supply workshops, retailers and dealers. Car owners are welcome to send an enquiry; we may point you to a partner workshop for fitting.

    How fast can you dispatch?

    Parts in stock at Mumbai or Delhi usually go out once your order is confirmed. Items we import to order take longer, and we tell you the expected date when we quote.

    Do you ship outside Mumbai and Delhi?

    Yes. We ship across India by surface express and share tracking once your order is dispatched.

    Do I get a GST invoice?

    Yes, every order comes with a GST tax invoice, so registered businesses can claim input credit.

    Can you source a part that isn't listed?

    Usually, yes. Our catalogue here is only a sample. Send the part number and we'll check our brands and suppliers in Europe.
